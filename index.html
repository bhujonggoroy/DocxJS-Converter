    Why Unicode Math for DOCX?

    Standard Word (.docx) files without complex third-party equation plugins fail to render raw LaTeX like \frac{a}{b} or \sqrt{x}. This app converts expressions into standard Unicode mathematical notation (e.g. x², √x, ∫, ∑, α, β) so your documents open perfectly in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Apple Pages, and mobile office suites without missing symbols or plugins.

    100% Privacy & Local Storage

    This app has no server backend and logs nothing. Your Gemini API key is stored only in your own browser's localStorage. All network requests go directly from your browser to Google's official Gemini API endpoint over encrypted HTTPS.
